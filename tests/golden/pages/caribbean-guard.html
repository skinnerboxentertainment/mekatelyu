Proposed Whappin Community Integration

Caribbean Guard

Building a stronger, safer community in the water.

A community-led aquatic-safety organization serving Costa Rica's South Caribbean through lifesaving training, swimming education, patrols, prevention, and emergency-response coordination.

Water Emergency?

  1. Call 911 — give the exact beach or landmark.

Call 911

Caribbean Guard operates a community emergency-alert network of 70+ trained members including lifeguards, surfers, swimmers, fishers, divers, and freedivers. When an emergency is reported, a protocol is activated and nearby qualified community members may respond.

Demo: Simulate an emergency alert to Caribbean Guard's network.

This is a demonstration of a future integration. In production, this would activate Caribbean Guard's emergency-alert network. No alert is actually sent.

Whappin does not monitor emergencies or dispatch rescuers. In an emergency, always call 911 first.

Programs

Lifesaving Club

Saturday morning training (location varies with ocean conditions) · Confirm with organizer

Continuing education, lifeguard courses, CPR and first-aid instructor training, swimming-instructor training, junior lifeguard / Nipper training, and ELLIS CPR training.

Swim Club

Punta Uva: Thursdays 7:15 AM | Playa Negra: Tuesdays & Fridays 4:00 PM · Free · Confirm with organizer

Free community swim sessions open to all.

Swim School

Free

Free swimming instruction for local children.

Programa Playa Organizada

Organized Beach Program including red-and-yellow-flag supervised bathing zones, survival lines, drone mapping, rescue stations, emergency plans, rapid-response groups, and CPR/lifeguard training for local workers.

Emergency-Alert Network

A community emergency group of 70+ trained members who may respond when a protocol is activated.
