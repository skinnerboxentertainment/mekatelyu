# Research Assignment: Strategic Evaluation of Take App Integration with Whappin

## Context

You are acting as an independent Product Strategy, Systems Architecture, UX, and Business Development reviewer.

Your objective is NOT to validate this proposal.

Your objective is to independently determine whether integrating Take App into the Whappin platform represents a strategically sound investment.

You are encouraged to disagree with assumptions made below.

Challenge every recommendation.

If there is a better solution than Take App, identify it.

If this idea is fundamentally flawed, explain why.

---

# Background

Whappin is a mobile-first community guide and discovery platform focused initially on Puerto Viejo de Talamanca, Costa Rica.

Current capabilities include:

- Local business discovery
- Rich business profiles
- Tourism information
- Community content
- Events
- Maps
- WhatsApp links
- Contact information
- Mobile-first UX

Whappin is intentionally NOT an e-commerce platform.

The long-term vision is to become the digital operating system for a local community.

---

# Initial Hypothesis

Take App may provide a significant amount of merchant functionality that would otherwise require substantial engineering effort.

Potential value includes:

- Commerce
- Online ordering
- Pickup
- Delivery
- Gift cards
- Merchant storefronts
- Product catalogs
- Payments
- WhatsApp ordering
- Customer communication

The assumption is that Whappin should remain focused on discovery while delegating commerce to specialized providers.

This assumption should be challenged.
